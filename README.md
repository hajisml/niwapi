# NiWapi

**Dual-Way Climate Risk Intelligence & Offline-Resilient Community Alert System**

Built for the Zone01 Kisumu GreenTech Hackathon 2026, Track 6: Climate Risk Intelligence & Resilience Platform.

NiWapi (Swahili for "Where is it?") is a two-way climate risk intelligence and early-warning platform for the Lake Victoria Basin and high-risk Kenyan regions. It bridges the gap between high-altitude satellite data and on-the-ground human reality through a bi-directional crisis network:

- **Inbound (community to hub):** citizens report localized hazards, such as flash floods, washed-out bridges, and submerged farms, via WhatsApp, Telegram, or offline USSD/SMS. Server-side models verify and map these reports.
- **Outbound (hub to community):** the platform ingests remote sensing and satellite telemetry, rainfall feeds, and localized weather forecasts, then broadcasts hyper-local hazard alerts back to affected communities via data-free web fallbacks, WhatsApp, and automated USSD/SMS.

To reach the common man, NiWapi is built with an offline-first, zero-data architecture: a Progressive Web App with offline caching, and USSD/SMS fallbacks for basic feature phones with no data connection.
